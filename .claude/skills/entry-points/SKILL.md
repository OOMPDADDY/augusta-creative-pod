# Category Entry Points

## The output

A ranked list of entry situations with a creative treatment against each.

## The prompt

```
Below are customer reviews, purchase triggers and any survey data on how people
came to buy.

SOURCE: [PASTE]

List the CATEGORY ENTRY POINTS: the recurring situations in which a person first
decides they need something in this category at all.

Emit only:

| Entry point | The situation | Evidence count | Seasonal? | Opening shot |

Seasonal: YES with the period, or NO.
Opening shot: one line describing what the first two seconds of an ad built for
this entry point would show.

Rank by evidence count. Do not invent an entry point that has no evidence.
```

## Why this is not persona work

A persona describes who someone is. An entry point describes when they became a
buyer, and the second one is far more actionable, because it can be filmed. Two
people with nothing demographically in common who both just moved apartment are
in the same market. The apartment is the asset, not the demographics.

## What to do with it

Each entry point with three or more pieces of evidence deserves its own concept.
The opening shot column feeds `shot-sheet` directly. Seasonal rows go on the
calendar rather than into the current set.
