# Angle Stress Test

## The output

A kill report per argument. It either survives or it does not.

## The prompt

```
Below is a funded argument, its proof, and the category context.

ARGUMENT: [PASTE]

Attack it. You are a skeptical buyer who has been sold to before, and a
competitor who wants this to fail. Work through all six:

1. THE SO WHAT      if a buyer said "so what", what is the answer, and is it in
                    the argument already or does it need another asset
2. THE PROOF HOLE   what would a skeptic ask for that the proof does not cover
3. THE MIRROR       could a competitor make this exact claim tomorrow, and if
                    so what makes ours different
4. THE COMMENT      write the single most damaging comment this ad would get
5. THE SCROLL       is the claim legible in the first two seconds, or does it
                    need eight
6. THE SUBSTITUTE   what does the buyer do instead if they believe us but do
                    not buy today

Then one verdict line: "SURVIVES" or "FAILS ON: [which test]" with one sentence.

Be harsh. A soft pass here is expensive later.
```

## Test four earns its place

Writing the worst comment the ad will receive is the fastest honesty mechanism
available. If the comment is unanswerable, the argument is not ready, and you
have learned it for free instead of learning it in the comments under live
spend. Keep the comment. Give it to whoever writes the response.

## What to do with it

FAILS results go back to `argument-brief` with the failing test named, not to
the bin. Most failures are fixable by narrowing the claim rather than dropping
it. Test three failures are the exception, because they mean the argument
belongs to the category rather than to the brand.
