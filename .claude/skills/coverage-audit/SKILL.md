# Coverage Audit

## What it answers

Not "how are we doing" but "what have we never once said."

## The prompt

```
Below are the claims customers repeat, and every asset currently live.

CUSTOMER CLAIMS: [PASTE SEAT 1 TABLE]
LIVE ASSETS: [PASTE, WITH DESCRIPTIONS]

Emit ONLY a table:
CLAIM | HOW OFTEN CUSTOMERS SAY IT | ASSETS ARGUING IT | GAP?

GAP?: YES where customers repeat a claim often and we have fewer than 2 assets
arguing it.

Then one line: which single claim has the highest customer frequency and the
lowest asset count. No commentary.
```

## Why this usually explains a plateau

A plateau rarely means the creative is bad. It usually means the account has
been arguing three claims well for a long time while a fourth claim, one
customers raise constantly, has never been tested at all.
